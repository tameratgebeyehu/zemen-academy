# Google Play reviewer access

Reviewer credentials must be entered only in **Play Console → App content → App access**. Never commit passwords, reset codes, session tokens, or a real reviewer email to Git.

## Accounts to prepare

Create two dedicated, non-student accounts immediately before submission:

1. **Free reviewer account** — active, Grade 9, no Premium entitlement.
2. **Premium reviewer account** — active Premium entitlement lasting at least 30 days beyond the expected review date.

Use unique strong passwords. Mark the accounts clearly in the private spreadsheet administration workflow, and delete or rotate them after the review is complete.

## Text for Play Console App access

Zemen Academy supports guest use and signed-in learning. No OTP, QR code, geographic restriction, or external device is required for the supplied reviewer accounts.

**Free path**

1. Launch the app and choose Sign in.
2. Enter the free reviewer credentials supplied in this Play Console field.
3. Open Quizzes, choose a Grade 9 Unit 1 quiz, and test Instant and Exam modes.
4. Open Profile → Account deletion to verify the in-app deletion path.

**Premium path**

1. Sign out, then sign in with the Premium reviewer credentials supplied here.
2. Open Profile → Premium access to view the existing entitlement and expiration date.
3. Open a Premium-locked quiz or note. The Play build contains no purchase, price, bank-detail, or payment-request flow.

**Device policy note**

Each account supports one phone and one tablet. Before submission, release any old device rows for both reviewer accounts so Google's review devices can sign in. If Google reports a device-limit error, release the review installation promptly and reply in Play Console.

## Pre-submission verification

- Test both accounts on a clean physical Android installation.
- Confirm the free account cannot open Premium content.
- Confirm the Premium account can open Premium content and its entitlement does not expire during review.
- Confirm reviewer credentials are present only in Play Console, not release notes, source code, screenshots, or Git history.
